# About Me

<p class="about-copy">I am <strong>Wanhao Liu (刘万昊)</strong>, a Ph.D. student in Information and Communication Engineering at the <a class="org-chip org-chip--ustc" href="https://www.ustc.edu.cn/"><img src="/images/logos/ustc.png" alt="">University of Science and Technology of China</a>, advised by <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=pw_0Z_UAAAAJ">Prof. Wanli Ouyang</a> and <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=7sFMIKoAAAAJ">Prof. Houqiang Li</a>. I also conduct AI for Science research with the <a class="org-chip org-chip--shlab" href="https://www.shlab.org.cn/"><img src="/images/logos/shlab.png" alt="">Shanghai AI Laboratory</a>, where I am co-advised by <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=RQqws5gAAAAJ">Dr. Yuqiang Li</a>. I am currently a research intern in the Foundation Model Team of <a class="org-chip org-chip--tencent" href="https://www.tencent.com/">Tencent</a> TEG through the Qingyun Program.</p>

<p class="about-copy">Prior to this, I studied Polymer Physics and Chemistry at USTC, where I worked at the Hefei National Research Center for Physical Sciences at the Microscale with Prof. Guangming Liu, before transferring directly into the Ph.D. program. I received my B.S. in Chemistry (National Science Base Program) from <a class="org-chip org-chip--ouc" href="https://www.ouc.edu.cn/"><img src="/images/logos/ouc.png" alt="">Ocean University of China</a>.</p>

<p class="about-copy">My research interests focus on <strong>LLMs for Scientific Discovery, Multimodal LLM Evaluation, and AI for Materials Science</strong>.</p>

<p class="collaboration-line"><strong>Collaboration:</strong> I am always happy to discuss AI for Science, automated scientific discovery, and LLM evaluation. If our interests overlap, please feel free to <a href="mailto:liuwanhao@mail.ustc.edu.cn">contact me</a>.</p>
